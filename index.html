Project context & asset acknowledgments

Isaac Twin is a custom simulation and data collection platform built on NVIDIA Isaac Sim and PhysX, with Nav2 navigation and LeRobot-format episode output. Robot assets and task design draw on the RoboTwin ecosystem; individual robot assets retain their upstream provenance. Room appearance and collision assets originate from SpatialVerse InteriorGS, scene 0267_840790. Demonstration videos are recordings from this platform.

Seven configurations are demonstrated on RGB block sorting. G2 mobile manipulation is demonstrated separately. These selected successful episodes establish implemented workflows, not a universal success-rate claim.
